How to Use Natz Market

1. Creating an Account

To create an account, click on the "Login" link in the navigation bar. On the login page, click the "Register here" link. Fill out the registration form with your personal information and click "Register". To log in, enter your email and password on the login page and click "Login".

2. Browsing Products

You can browse products on the "Shop" page. You can use the search bar to find specific products, or use the category and sort filters to narrow down your search. The products are displayed in a grid, and you can click on a product to view its details.

3. Product Details and Quick View

Clicking on a product card will take you to the product details page, where you can see more information about the product. Alternatively, you can click the "Quick View" button on the product card to see a summary of the product in a modal window.

4. Adding to Cart

To add a product to your cart, click the "Add to Cart" button on the product card or on the product details page. A confirmation message will appear, and the product will be added to your cart.

5. Managing the Cart

Click on the "Cart" link in the navigation bar to view your cart. Here you can see all the products you have added. You can change the quantity of each product using the "+" and "-" buttons, or remove a product from the cart by clicking the "Remove" button.

6. Checkout

When you are ready to purchase, click the "Checkout" button in your cart. You will be taken to the checkout page, where you need to fill in your shipping address and choose a payment method. After confirming your order, it will be placed.

7. Account Management

In the "Account" page, you can view your profile information and your order history.

8. Settings

In the "Settings" page, you can update your personal information, such as your name, email, phone number, and date of birth. You can also switch between the light and dark themes for the website.

9. Support

If you need help, you can visit the "Support" page. Here you can find answers to frequently asked questions or contact us using the contact form.
